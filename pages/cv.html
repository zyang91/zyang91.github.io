September 2025 – April 2026

Telework Intensity and Daily Travel Behavior

Independent Researcher · Master's Thesis

University of Pennsylvania · Faculty Advisor: Erick Guerra

How does daily telework intensity reshape weekday trip-making?

View details Hide details

Key work

  • Analyzed Minneapolis–St. Paul Travel Behavior Inventory data from 2019, 2021, and 2023.
  • Modeled commuting and non-work travel behavior using count and ordinal models and matching approaches.
  • Identified a nonlinear relationship between telework intensity and trip generation.
  • Examined the implications of hybrid work for transportation demand and planning.

Output

How Daily Telework Intensity Reshapes Weekday Trip-Making: Evidence from the Twin Cities, Transportation Research Part A, under review.

September 2025 – August 2026

Impact of SEPTA Service Reduction and Budget Constraints on Philadelphia Traffic Speed

Data Analyst · Research Assistant · Coauthor

University of Pennsylvania · Faculty Advisors: Gilles Duranton & Erick Guerra

What happens to urban road traffic when transit service is abruptly reduced?

View details Hide details

Key work

  • Constructed a traffic-speed panel containing approximately 40,000 OD pairs and more than 120,000 observations.
  • Developed measures of transit-cut exposure.
  • Applied quasi-experimental approaches using Philadelphia as the treatment city and Boston and Chicago as comparison cities.
  • Integrated GTFS data to evaluate changes in transit travel time.
  • Examined spatial heterogeneity across radial and non-radial travel markets.

Metro Project of Bogotá

Research Assistant

University of Pennsylvania / University of the Andes · Faculty Advisors: C. Erik Vergel-Tovar & Erick Guerra

How do residents perceive Bogotá's metro project, and what shapes their attitudes?

View details Hide details
